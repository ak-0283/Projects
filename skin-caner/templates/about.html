About SkinCare AI

Supporting Early Awareness with AI

SkinCare AI is a deep-learning powered web application that helps users screen skin lesions using dermoscopic images. The goal is to provide fast, clear predictions that support early awareness and informed next steps.

What It Does

Upload a skin image and receive an instant classification across three lesion types: BKL, MEL, and NV. Each prediction is paired with a confidence score for transparency.

How It Works

The model is built on EfficientNet and trained on curated dermoscopic datasets. Uploaded images are preprocessed, normalized, and then passed through the neural network for prediction.

Why It Matters

Early awareness can make a real difference. This tool is designed to complement medical consultation by helping users recognize patterns that may need attention.

Medical Disclaimer

SkinCare AI is an educational tool and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you have concerns, consult a certified dermatologist.
